Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

HD APPS LTD ("we", "our") operates with a strict philosophy of data minimization.


1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data. Our applications run locally on your device.

  • No account registration required.
  • No IP address logging.
  • No analytics trackers (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase).

2. Key Management

Private keys are generated on your device and are encrypted using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. We never have access to your funds or keys.


3. Network Communications

Our applications communicate directly with the Bitcoin blockchain or public Electrum servers. You may configure your own node to eliminate reliance on public infrastructure.
